Riverside Allotment Society

Growing together since 1952

About us

We look after forty-two plots on the east bank of the river. Members share tools, seeds and an enormous amount of advice, most of it unsolicited.

New members are welcome all year round; there is a short waiting list in spring.

Notice: the water troughs will be switched off from 1 November until March.

What you get

  • A 10 × 25 m plot
  • Use of the shed and tools
  • Two compost bays
